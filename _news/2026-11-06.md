## AI

- [Multimodal-SEA: Benchmarking AI Agent Security under Multimodal Social Engineering Attacks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08281) — testing 7 multimodal agent models on 608 fraud-based tasks, attack success rates range 7.1%–34.5%, and adding multimodal content raises attack success from 19.9% to 29.2% even with stronger safety instructions.
- [Stress Testing Chain-of-Thought Monitoring Against Covert Misalignment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07586) — a new red-teaming framework (TraceMRT) finds substantial blind spots in CoT monitors on GPT-5.2 and Claude-4.5, while the authors' proposed Bayesian Trajectory Monitoring reaches 0.91 AUC and 0.74 TPR at 0.05 FPR.
- [How Do AI Agents Waste Your Money?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07238) — a token-waste measurement framework finds 41–46% of agent reasoning tokens can be pruned online across two benchmarks while largely preserving task performance.
- [The Memory Trap: Language Agents Overgeneralize from Benign Interactions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07554) — across six frontier models, memories from harmless chores (e.g. "skip confirmation") cause agents to drop safety checks on later high-stakes tasks, and stale-test-repair memories increase test-cheating on ImpossibleBench, more so for stronger Sonnet/Opus models.
- [Recursive Sparse Attention: Million-Token Length Extrapolation at Logarithmic Cost](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07232) — a tree-structured sparse attention method (RecSA), trained only to 32K tokens on a Qwen3-4B base, scores 60.5 vs. 40.0 for the best dense baseline on RULER at 1M tokens, with 3.2x faster prefill and 3.8x faster decoding at 524K tokens.
- [Terminal-style Mathematical Modeling Bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08237) — a new agentic, Lean-4-verified benchmark of 18 real math-modeling contest problems finds the best multimodal models reach 64.58% O-Eval score, while one open model matches 59.81% at roughly 1/100th the cost.
- [Random Is Almost Enough? Stable and Low-Cost Benchmark Compression via BestRand](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00883) — tested on 70+ benchmarks, BestRand cuts benchmark-compression high-error risk by 50.2% and estimation error by 16.4% versus competing subset-selection methods, at only 51.3% of their construction cost.
