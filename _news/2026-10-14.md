## AI

- [Hugging Face Papers — Vela: User Behavior as Supervision Sparks LLM Intelligence](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.14709) — Fine-tuning Qwen3.6-35B-A3B on ~75K tasks derived from 100K users' anonymized behavior logs lifts VelaBench from 51.5 to 63.0 (+11.5 pts), gains +6.2 pts on average across 4 user-related out-of-distribution benchmarks, and still improves general benchmarks by +4.0 pts on average — user-behavior supervision transfers to general capability, not just the target task.
- [Hugging Face Papers — DoSA: Dots Sparse Attention](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.13990) — Reframes sparse-attention top-K token selection as needing only enough evidence to reject a candidate, not its full score, ruling out most keys via bound certificates before full scoring; this makes the indexer up to 2.74x faster than SGLang and cuts time-to-first-token up to ~1.9x on GLM-5.2 and DeepSeek-V3.2 at long context.
- [Hugging Face Papers — UltraBERT: Beyond the Limit of BERT Pre-Training](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.15028) — Finds encoder masking ratio creates a tradeoff missed by prior work (~5% masking favors classification, ~50% favors retrieval) and resolves it with one encoder using separate LM heads per ratio, reaching 91.0 on GLUE and 47.7 nDCG@10 on BEIR — claimed first encoder state-of-the-art on both classification and retrieval simultaneously.

## General

- [ThoughtWorks Insights — The real AI project at your airport isn't an AI project](https://www.thoughtworks.com/insights/blog/technology-strategy/the-real-ai-project-at-your-airport-isnt-an-ai-project) — Argues the blocker for agentic AI at airports isn't the model but fragmented operational data (ops database, A-CDM feed, ops plan) across systems, so agents acting on it will confidently decide on conflicting information — the first real investment has to be unifying that data layer, not picking an AI model.
