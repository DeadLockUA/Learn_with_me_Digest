- [What Effort Level Should I Choose? On the Economics of Reasoning Effort in LLM Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34491) — Across four benchmarks and both proprietary and open-weight models, higher reasoning effort often fails tasks lower effort solves and costs up to 42x more on easy tasks; escalating effort only after a verified failure matches or beats top-effort accuracy at lower cost for most proprietary models.
