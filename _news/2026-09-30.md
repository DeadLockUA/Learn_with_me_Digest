## AI

- [Hugging Face Papers](https://huggingface.co/papers/date/2026-09-30) — Top paper "LongLive-Plug" (NVIDIA) proposes distilling video-generation capabilities once per model family into reusable LoRA modules that plug into compatible downstream models training-free, instead of re-distilling each one from scratch.
- [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37914) — EleutherAI researchers show training-data attribution can identify which fine-tuning examples drive emergent misalignment, letting it be amplified or suppressed — though attribution transfers within a model family but not across families.
- [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38107) — Study of verifiable grade-school math finds models often reach correct final answers via reasoning traces that fail step-by-step verification, meaning a correct answer isn't evidence the reasoning itself was valid.
