## Officials say:

- [Codex CLI 0.172.0](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/changelog) — adds Amazon Bedrock Runtime as a provider choice during app-server setup, documents migration to GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol (endpoint and tool-calling requirements), and makes `codex exec resume` restore saved sandbox permissions, working directory, and workspace roots.

## AI

- [Simon Willison's Weblog — datasette-agent 0.7a1](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Nov/24/datasette-agent/) — new release adds an iframe side panel to chat sessions that can navigate between pages, including by fragment hash, for better interplay with other Datasette plugins.
- [Hugging Face Papers — Self-WSP: Can Language Models Improve Themselves Through Weight-Space Programming?](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.28833) — proposes having language models edit their own weights directly via a learned weight-space program, instead of only through gradient updates.
- [Hugging Face Papers — WeightCode: Installing New Capabilities into Neural Networks via Weight-Space Programming](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.25476) — shows new capabilities can be installed into a trained network by writing directly to its weight space rather than fine-tuning it.

## General

- [Stack Overflow Blog — Agent swarms need more than a message board](https://stackoverflow.blog/2026/11/24/agent-swarms-need-more-than-a-message-board/) — argues multi-agent "swarms" are a distributed-systems problem, needing dedicated infrastructure for discovery, identity, observability, and sandboxing, not an ad hoc shared scratchpad.
