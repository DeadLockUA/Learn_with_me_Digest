## AI

- [arXiv:2610.30113 — Quant-Arena](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30113) — New benchmark testing whether LLM agents can run end-to-end quantitative trading research on real A-share data; none of 9 evaluated models beat the equal-weight market in the sealed 2024 test year.
- [Hugging Face Papers — Automating ML for Science](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.28763) — Frontier coding agents can beat published best results on scientific ML benchmarks, but the gains come mainly from routine engineering tricks rather than domain insight, and lightweight planning/experiment-management skills substantially boost performance.
- [Hugging Face Papers — Harness Scaling for Generalizable Agentic RL](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.29458) — Training an agentic model across structured variations of its execution harness, rather than one fixed harness, improves generalization to unseen harness configurations, including external black-box harnesses.
