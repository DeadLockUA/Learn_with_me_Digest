## Development

- [InfoQ](https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/09/kubernetes-1-37/) — Kubernetes 1.37 ships, graduating the Metrics API to general availability and moving Rootless Kubelet to beta, plus default-enabled HPA scale-to-zero for GPU/AI-ML workloads.

## AI

- [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.22000) — RecreationWorld's new computer-use agent benchmark shows the top model, GPT-6 Astra, leads with 58.1% overall accuracy but passes the full programmatic test suite on only 2.8% of its 250 tasks, exposing a gap between visual fidelity and functional correctness.
- [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.22043) — A parameter-free "Memory Decision Layer" for LLM agents cuts hallucination rates by about 56% under conflicting-memory conditions, by decoupling retrieved-fact confidence from consistency at ~0.14ms overhead per decision.
- [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20942) — Training LLM peer-reviewers on prior AI-generated reviews causes "scientific-judgment collapse" (compressed rating distributions, reduced semantic diversity); the authors' TrustReviewer mitigates it via curated training data and activation steering.
