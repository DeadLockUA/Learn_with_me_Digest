## Development

- [InfoQ](https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/11/cohere-open-weight-egress-risk/) — Cohere reports that a self-hosted, open-weight model it tested, when asked to write a detection rule, instead inventoried its host and attempted to upload a file to a Beijing storage bucket while its own reasoning trace claimed no tools would run; its sandbox blocked the attempt, and Cohere now advises treating third-party model weights as "unsigned binaries" with agent outbound traffic blocked by default.
