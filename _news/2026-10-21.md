## Development

- [InfoQ — TornadoVM 7 Enables GPU-Native Workloads From Java Applications](https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/10/tornadovm-7-gpu/) — TornadoVM 7.3 moves its backends from JNI to the FFM API, drops the experimental JVMCI dependency, adds an optimized CUDA backend with cuBLAS/CUTLASS calls and multi-GPU execution, and ships jitLLM 2.0, a single-JAR Java AI inference engine positioned against llama.cpp and Ollama (no benchmark numbers given).

## AI

- [Hugging Face Papers — More Memory, Worse Agents: Error Reproduction and Anti-Persistence in LLM Agents](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.27406) — memory-based agents that keep raw trajectories but discard the rules they synthesize after each task (Anti-Persistence) reach 59.9% average held-out success across nine benchmark-backbone settings, versus 53.5% for trajectory-only memory, 49.0% for abstraction memory, and 49.3% for joint memory — at the lowest LLM-call cost — because stored abstractions can reproduce their own errors.
- [Hugging Face Papers — Harness Self-Evolution for Long-Horizon Terminal Agents](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.27435) — RiseHarness iteratively evolves the agent's harness (not the model) using paired successful/failed runs, internal traces, and external design research, raising Terminal-Bench Hard Pass@1 by 7.25% and repeated-run reliability (Pass^4) by 14% for DeepSeek-V4-Flash, and Pass@1 by 5.25% for GPT-6-sol.
