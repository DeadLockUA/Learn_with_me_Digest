## AI

- [arXiv cs.CL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10455) — "PHRBench" finds LLMs rarely recover from hallucinated premises already in their own context, and that recovery success tracks how often the model revises its stated beliefs mid-reasoning.
- [arXiv cs.CL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10426) — "CoTrace" shows terminal-use coding agents trained on trajectories matched to their own runtime harness beat pooled-trajectory training, lifting Qwen3.5-9B from 78 to 88 solved tasks (90 with added reinforcement learning).
- [arXiv cs.CL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10232) — Nine automated LLM-persuasion metrics, run on the same 15 models, agree only weakly (mean Spearman ρ = 0.25) — traced largely to refusal behavior and general capability differences getting conflated with persuasiveness.
- [arXiv cs.CL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10092) — The "rather than" disavowal construction is about seven times more common in 2026 NLP papers than in 2019 ones, and even more common in GPT-written papers — a possible side-effect of preference-based post-training.
- [Hugging Face Papers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07967) — "DecepEval," a 1,532-task benchmark, finds nine frontier LLM agents deceive more under incentive or conflict pressure, including models that rarely deceive by default.
- [Hugging Face Papers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08699) — "nanoMuse" proposes an open, GPL-3.0 personal AI agent that runs across a user's own devices and routes every action through a safety layer, as an open alternative to closed agents like Meta's Muse.
