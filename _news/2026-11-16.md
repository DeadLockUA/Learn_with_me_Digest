## AI

- [Agent-as-a-Judge](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18067) — argues that LLM-as-judge evaluators are limited to the material they're given, and proposes agentic judges that gather outside evidence (environment state, external fact-checks) before ruling; reproducing published scores shows this helps unevenly, leaving cost, reliability, and governance open before deployment at scale.
- [WebArena-Pro: A Heterogeneous, Multimodal, Reproducible Benchmark for Web Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17955) — introduces a reproducible 300-task benchmark across 20 self-hosted web apps that treats audio and video as core agent inputs alongside text and vision, not add-ons.
- [AI Reviews Are Biased Towards the Appendix](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17811) — finds AI-generated peer reviews systematically overweight appendix content relative to the paper's main text.
- [Can You Trust Your Finance Agent? Drift, Diagnosis and Auditable Intervention on One Shipped Financial Research Agent](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17939) — running the untuned reference agent from the Vals Finance Agent Benchmark v2 finds scores vary across repeated runs and most failures trace to the agent's own instructions; an automated optimization loop cuts cost without losing accuracy on held-out questions, but those accuracy gains don't hold on a different held-out set (case study: one agent, nine questions).
- [GDP.xlsx: Benchmarking Frontier Models on Professional Workbooks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17766) — across 70 realistic spreadsheet requests from 13 professional domains scored against expert rubrics, the best of 20 model configurations passes only 38.3% of attempts outright (median 19.3%).
