## Development

- [Netflix Tech Blog](https://netflixtechblog.com/trading-a-cloud-identity-for-your-own-workload-attestation-on-managed-compute-516d5a29b252) — Netflix lets Apache Spark workloads on managed compute swap the cloud provider's identity for Netflix's own internal workload identity, combining signed instance metadata with pre-signed-URL verification so managed-compute jobs get the same attested identity as self-run infrastructure.

## AI

- [arXiv cs.CL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02206) — KaliBench: 8,504 query-command pairs across 1,642 Kali Linux security tools; no open-weight model exceeds 42% exact-command accuracy unrestricted, but SFT+RL lifts an 8B model to parity with a 685B one.
- [arXiv cs.CL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02163) — AutoCompact: training coding agents to decide for themselves when to compact context raises pass rate +9.2% on SWE-bench Verified and +5.0% on SWE-PolyBench Verified, holding across 16K-256K context windows.
- [arXiv cs.CL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02193) — Hierarchical Continuous Diffusion LMs (HC-DLM): unifying discrete token generation with continuous latent denoising beats same-size discrete and continuous diffusion baselines on Sudoku, Countdown, and LM1B perplexity.
- [Hugging Face Papers](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.00906) — ActiveSaddler: adapting the training curriculum jointly with agent-harness optimization adds +4.4 and +7.5 pp Pass@1 over fixed-curriculum baselines, framing curriculum design as a new axis of harness optimization.
- [Hugging Face Papers](https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.37200) — Adaptive Reward Routing: routing multi-reward RL updates across layers by cross-modal interaction, and rebalancing rewards so dominant ones don't suppress weaker ones, improves audio-video diffusion quality, semantic consistency, and A/V sync over RL baselines.
