## Development

- [InfoQ](https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/11/discord-rust-rewrite-gpui-zed/) — Discord is rewriting its desktop (replacing Electron) and mobile (replacing React Native) clients in Rust, using Zed's GPU-accelerated GPUI framework for desktop rendering, reporting early internal results of ~290MB RAM use and near-instant cold start but acknowledging GPUI lacks built-in text layout, accessibility, and RTL support that browsers provide.
