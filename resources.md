# resources.md — Authoritative Source List

Curated reference list, not a digest entry. Top 10 per recurring topic
(§2, AGENTS.md) plus one cross-topic block. Selection bar: primary or
practitioner-authored, actively maintained, no vendor marketing or
affiliate content farms. Each source appears once; cross-topic ones live
in the General block.

## Testing

1. [Google Testing Blog](https://testing.googleblog.com/) — Google's internal testing practice.
2. [Software Engineering at Google — Testing chapters](https://abseil.io/resources/swe-book) — free book; test sizes, flakiness, larger testing.
3. [Martin Fowler — Testing](https://martinfowler.com/testing/) — test pyramid, test doubles, Practical Test Pyramid.
4. [James Bach — Satisfice](https://www.satisfice.com/blog) — Rapid Software Testing, exploratory testing.
5. [Michael Bolton — DevelopSense](https://developsense.com/blog) — testing vs checking, critical thinking for testers.
6. [Lisa Crispin & Janet Gregory — Agile Testing](https://agiletester.ca/) — agile testing quadrants, whole-team quality.
7. [Gojko Adzic](https://gojko.net/) — Specification by Example, impact mapping.
8. [Ministry of Testing](https://www.ministryoftesting.com/) — largest practitioner community; articles, talks.
9. [Software Testing Weekly](https://softwaretestingweekly.com/) — curated weekly newsletter.
10. [ISTQB Glossary](https://glossary.istqb.org/) — standard terminology reference (use for definitions, not opinion).

## Development

1. [Martin Fowler](https://martinfowler.com/) — architecture, refactoring, patterns.
2. [MDN Web Docs](https://developer.mozilla.org/) — web platform reference.
3. [ThoughtWorks Technology Radar](https://www.thoughtworks.com/radar) — tool/technique adoption trends, twice yearly.
4. [Google Engineering Practices](https://google.github.io/eng-practices/) — code review and CL author guides.
5. [ACM Queue](https://queue.acm.org/) — peer-reviewed practitioner articles.
6. [InfoQ](https://www.infoq.com/) — architecture and engineering news, conference talks.
7. [GitHub Blog — Engineering](https://github.blog/category/engineering/) — real-world engineering practice at scale.
8. [Netflix Tech Blog](https://netflixtechblog.com/) — distributed systems, reliability case studies.
9. [Dan Luu](https://danluu.com/) — data-driven technical analysis and postmortems.
10. [Julia Evans](https://jvns.ca/) — systems fundamentals explained from first principles.

## AI

1. [Anthropic Research](https://www.anthropic.com/research) — primary research papers and model cards.
2. [OpenAI Research](https://openai.com/research/) — primary research papers and system cards.
3. [Google DeepMind Research](https://deepmind.google/research/) — primary research publications.
4. [arXiv cs.CL / cs.AI / cs.LG](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.CL/recent) — preprints; primary source for any benchmark claim.
5. [Hugging Face Papers & Blog](https://huggingface.co/papers) — open-model ecosystem, daily paper feed (replaced Papers with Code).
6. [Simon Willison's Weblog](https://simonwillison.net/) — hands-on LLM tooling analysis, near-daily.
7. [Import AI (Jack Clark)](https://importai.substack.com/) — weekly research digest with policy context.
8. [Interconnects (Nathan Lambert)](https://www.interconnects.ai/) — open-model training, RLHF, post-training analysis.
9. [Epoch AI](https://epoch.ai/) — compute, cost, and capability trend data.
10. [METR](https://metr.org/) — independent evaluations, incl. AI impact on developer productivity.

> Recency reminder (AGENTS.md §6): any AI-related citation used in an entry
> must be ≤6 months old (prefer ≤3), regardless of which topic the entry is
> filed under. Company research pages are primary sources but not neutral —
> distinguish fact from vendor framing.

## Processes

1. [DORA](https://dora.dev/) — State of DevOps research, delivery metrics.
2. [Scrum Guide](https://scrumguides.org/) — the canonical Scrum text, not a vendor summary.
3. [Martin Fowler — Process Theory](https://martinfowler.com/tags/process%20theory.html) — agile foundations, cycle time, YAGNI.
4. [Team Topologies](https://teamtopologies.com/) — team structure, cognitive load, Conway's law.
5. [Google re:Work](https://rework.withgoogle.com/) — Google's people/team research (Project Aristotle).
6. [Will Larson — Irrational Exuberance](https://lethain.com/) — engineering management and org design.
7. [Rands in Repose](https://randsinrepose.com/) — engineering leadership essays.
8. [Agile Alliance](https://www.agilealliance.org/resources/) — agile practice library, glossary.
9. [InfoQ — Culture & Methods](https://www.infoq.com/culture-methods/) — org/process case studies.
10. [Atlassian Agile Coach](https://www.atlassian.com/agile) — vendor, but the clearest fundamentals primer; use for basics only.

## General (cross-topic)

Sources that regularly span two or more of the four topics.

1. [The Pragmatic Engineer (Gergely Orosz)](https://newsletter.pragmaticengineer.com/) — dev + processes + AI; industry deep dives.
2. [Kent Beck — Tidy First?](https://tidyfirst.substack.com/) — dev + testing + processes; TDD/XP origin.
3. [Hillel Wayne](https://buttondown.com/hillelwayne) — dev + testing; formal methods, empirical software engineering.
4. [Charity Majors](https://charity.wtf/) — dev + processes; observability, engineering culture.
5. [Communications of the ACM](https://cacm.acm.org/) — all four; peer-reviewed plus practitioner columns.
6. [ThoughtWorks Insights](https://www.thoughtworks.com/insights) — dev + processes + AI.
7. [Software Engineering Radio](https://se-radio.net/) — all four; IEEE-backed podcast with expert guests.
8. [Stack Overflow Blog & Developer Survey](https://survey.stackoverflow.co/) — dev + AI; annual survey data.
9. [Lenny's Newsletter](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/) — processes + AI; product and team practice.
10. [Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/) — all four; aggregator, use for discovery only, never as a citation.
